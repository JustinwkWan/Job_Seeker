Takeaways: **1 in 4 "new grad" postings this fall is aimed at the class of 2027.** These are recruiting-season reqs for 2027 start dates (TikTok, Databricks, Atlassian, DoorDash, Pinterest, Sentry, Replit, C3 AI). MS requirements and clearance walls are rare. Most postings (≈75%) ask for 0–1 years or say "new grad" outright, so experience isn't the main filter; graduation timing is.

Together they account for 72 "missing keyword" hits in this week's scoring.
2. **Move "distributed systems" and "data structures & algorithms" into Skills (29% each).** Both are only in your bullets. A recruiter skimming the Skills line misses them.
3. **Learn Go and ship one small Go project.** At 35% it's the #4 skill overall and the most frequent true gap in the daily notes (Twitch, Ketch, Rocket, TikTok infra). A Go port of your Redis Pub/Sub worker pool or WebSocket server would cover Go + concurrency + distributed systems in one bullet.
